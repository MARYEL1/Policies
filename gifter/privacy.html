Gifter Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Gifter does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server that receives your data.

What Gifter stores

The people, gifts, ideas, notes, photos, occasions and gift history you add. These are stored on your device and, when iCloud is on, synced through your private iCloud account, run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access them.

Shared wishlists

If you share a list, Apple's iCloud sharing makes the list's contents available to the people you invite, and their changes come back to you. You choose who is invited and can stop sharing at any time. People you invite see the name on their Apple Account as iCloud shows it.

Saving from Safari

When you share a web page to Gifter, the page is loaded to suggest a title and a price, as a browser would. Nothing else is sent.

Reminders

Birthday and occasion reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a server.

Photos and camera

Gifter only reads the photos you pick, and uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for a gift.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from them.

Backups and exports

Backup files and exported lists are created only when you ask, and go where you choose to save or send them.

Deleting your data

Delete All Data in Gifter's Settings removes your people, gifts and history from the app and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

Children

Gifter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
