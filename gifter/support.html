Common questions

My gifts don't appear on my other device.

Sync uses your private iCloud. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on, and that Gifter is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

I don't get birthday reminders.

Allow notifications for Gifter in the Settings app → Notifications → Gifter, and check that the person has a birthday set.

Gifter isn't in the Safari share sheet.

Tap Share on a web page, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More and turn on Gifter.

My purchase isn't showing.

Open Settings in Gifter and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I move my data or keep a copy?

Use Export Backup in Settings. A backup contains your gifts, people, gift history and settings, and can be brought back with Import Backup.
