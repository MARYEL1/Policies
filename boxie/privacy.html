Boxie Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Boxie does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server that receives your data.

What Boxie stores

The boxes, items, tags, descriptions and photos you add. These are stored on your device and, when iCloud is on, synced through your private iCloud account, run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access them.

Shared inventories

If you share your inventory, Apple's iCloud sharing makes its boxes, items, tags and photos available to the people you invite, who can view and edit them. You choose who is invited and can stop sharing or leave a shared inventory at any time. People you invite see the name on their Apple Account as iCloud shows it.

Photos and camera

Boxie only reads the photos you pick, and uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo of a box or an item.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from them.

Backups and exports

Backup files and exported lists are created only when you ask, and go where you choose to save or send them.

Deleting your data

Delete All Data in Boxie's Settings removes your inventory from the app and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

Children

Boxie does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
