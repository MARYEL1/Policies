Common questions

My boxes don't appear on my other device.

Sync uses your private iCloud. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on, and that Boxie is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

How do I share my inventory?

Open Settings in Boxie, go to the shared storage section and invite someone. They need an Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Changes sync for everyone in the shared inventory.

My purchase isn't showing.

Open Settings in Boxie and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I keep a copy of my inventory?

Use Export Backup in Settings. A backup contains your boxes, items, tags and custom settings, and can be brought back with Import Backup.
