Support

Questions, problems or ideas for Asterism are welcome.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/MARYEL1/Policies/issues. Mention your device, the system version and the Asterism version (Settings → About Asterism), and what you expected to happen. Issues there are public, so leave out anything private.

Common questions

My library doesn't appear on my other device.

Sync uses your private iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Asterism is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. The first sync of a large library can take a while.

Asterism doesn't show up in the share sheet.

Open the share sheet, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn on Asterism. You can drag it closer to the front from there.

Organizing doesn't suggest titles or tags.

Suggestions on the device need Apple Intelligence to be available and turned on, with the device and Siri set to a supported language. You can also connect OpenAI or Claude in Settings, or skip organizing and add tags yourself. Activity, in the "more" menu, shows anything that is waiting or failed.

How do I connect a service such as TMDB, Discogs or Readwise?

Go to Settings and pick the service. Each page explains where to get a key or token. Keys stay in this device's Keychain.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleted items go to Trash first. Swipe on an item in Trash to restore it.
