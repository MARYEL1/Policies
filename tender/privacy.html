Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Tender does not collect data about you. There is no Tender account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your recipes, bar and lists are not sent to the developer.

Where your data is stored

Your favorites, ratings, lists, home bar, saved filters, and your own recipes and photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, Tender syncs them between your devices through your private iCloud account, run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access them.

Recipe catalog and photos

Tender downloads its public recipe catalog from Google Firebase and recipe photos from GitHub and the image hosts used by a recipe's source. Like any web request, these services see your IP address and what was requested. Nothing about you or your own content is sent with these downloads. See Firebase's privacy information and the GitHub privacy statement.

Photos and camera

Tender only reads a photo you pick with Apple's photo picker, and only uses the camera when you choose to take a photo for a recipe.

Tender Pro

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Tender checks with Apple whether Pro is active. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Contributions you choose to send

If you use Contribute or Report a problem, Tender sends what you enter to the developer through Google Firebase for review. This includes the recipe or report, the display name you choose for credits, any source link and image credits, and a photo if you attach one. Photos are re-encoded and their embedded metadata, such as location, is removed. To keep your submissions apart from other people's, Firebase creates a random anonymous identifier for your installation, and Apple's App Attest confirms the request comes from the real Tender app. Neither is linked to your name or Apple Account.

If a contribution is published, its content, photo and the display name you gave become part of the public catalog. You can withdraw a contribution in the app while it is waiting for review.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, use Reset and delete local data in Settings, or remove the app. To remove the synced copy, delete Tender's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. To have a contribution or a support email deleted, email the developer.

Children

Tender does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They have no cookies, scripts or analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
