Support

Questions, problems, recipe corrections or ideas for Tender are welcome.

Contact

Email martin.stomberg@icloud.com. Mention your device, the system version and the Tender version (shown in Settings), and what you expected to happen. For a recipe, include its name.

Common questions

Recipes are missing or haven't updated.

Tender downloads the recipe catalog when it has an internet connection. Open the app while connected and give it a moment. Once downloaded, the catalog works offline. You can see the catalog version in Settings → Catalog.

My recipes don't appear on my other device.

Sync uses your private iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that Tender is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. The first sync can take a while.

I bought Tender Pro but it isn't unlocked.

Open Tender Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. Purchases are handled by Apple.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Use Manage subscription on the Tender Pro screen in the app, or go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before the next renewal date.

A recipe has a mistake.

Open the recipe and choose Report a problem, or email the recipe name and what should change.

How do I add my own photo?

When creating or editing your own recipe, turn on Include image and pick a photo or take one. Your own recipes need Tender Pro.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items in the app, or use Reset and delete local data in Settings. The downloaded recipe catalog stays available. See the privacy policy for iCloud and contributions.
